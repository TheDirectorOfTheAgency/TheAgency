# Home Service Growth Platform - Product Sketch

## The One-Liner
"The system that took a TV mounting business to $500k with 60% margins — now available for any home service."

---

## Who It's For
- Home service business owners doing $0-$500k
- Trades: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, garage doors, landscaping, painting, cleaning, roofing, etc.
- People who are great at their trade but struggling with marketing/growth

---

## The Problem
Most home service operators:
- Have a terrible website (or none)
- Don't understand SEO or Google Ads
- Compete on price instead of value
- Have few/no reviews
- Are stuck trading hours for dollars
- Don't know how to scale past themselves

---

## User Journey

### Step 1: Onboarding (5 min)
"Tell me about your business"
- What's your trade? (dropdown: plumbing, HVAC, garage doors, etc.)
- What area do you serve? (city/zip)
- How long in business?
- Current revenue range?
- Biggest challenge right now? (getting leads / converting leads / hiring / pricing)

### Step 2: Growth Assessment
AI analyzes their situation and produces:
- Current state summary
- Biggest opportunities
- Recommended 90-day focus
- Comparison to benchmarks ("businesses like yours typically see X")

### Step 3: The Playbook (Core Product)
Guided modules they work through:

**Module 1: Foundation**
- Positioning: How to stop being a commodity
- Pricing: How to charge premium and win
- Service area strategy: Where to focus

**Module 2: Website & SEO**
- Site structure that ranks
- Location pages strategy (the 20+ suburb approach)
- Content strategy
- Technical SEO basics
- AI helps generate page outlines, meta descriptions, etc.

**Module 3: Google Business Profile**
- Complete optimization walkthrough
- Photo strategy
- Post strategy
- Q&A seeding
- Review response templates

**Module 4: Reviews**
- Why reviews are everything
- System for generating reviews consistently
- How to respond (good and bad)
- Tools and automation

**Module 5: Google Ads**
- Campaign structure for local services
- Keyword strategy
- Ad copy templates
- Budget allocation
- How to read the data
- When to scale

**Module 6: Converting Leads**
- Phone script frameworks
- Pricing presentation
- Upsell strategies
- Follow-up sequences

### Step 4: Implementation
Each module has:
- Video/text training (your knowledge)
- AI assistant to answer questions
- Templates and swipe files
- Checklists
- "Do it for me" prompts (AI generates their location pages, ad copy, etc.)

### Step 5: Ongoing
- Weekly check-in prompts
- Metric tracking dashboard
- "What to focus on this week" recommendations
- Community (optional)

## The Ecosystem (Full Vision)

The software is just one piece. The full ecosystem:

### Tier 1: The Software ($29-49/mo)
**"My brain downloaded into AI"**
- For people who can't afford high-ticket coaching
- AI acts as their coach, powered by your knowledge
- Self-paced, scalable, accessible
- The funnel that feeds everything else

### Tier 2: Group Coaching ($2,000-5,000)
- 6-8 week program
- Weekly group calls
- Accountability and community
- For people who want more structure and access

### Tier 3: 1:1 Coaching ($5,000-15,000+)
- Direct access to you
- Custom strategy
- Done-with-you implementation
- Limited spots

### Tier 4: The Agency Live ($10,000-25,000+)
**The premium in-person experience in Minneapolis**

Part business intensive, part adventure:
- 2-3 day immersive experience
- Build out their website, GBP, Google Ads together
- James Bond / spy-themed experience
- Activities: shooting range, skydiving, flight lessons, etc.
- Leave with systems fully built + unforgettable memories

This is the "bucket list" tier. People talk about it, share it, it becomes marketing itself.

*Positioning: "Come to Minneapolis. Build your empire. Have the adventure of a lifetime."*

## Why This Ecosystem Works

1. **Software** captures the masses, generates recurring revenue, builds audience

Everyone enters through the software. The best ones ascend.
